## Brand & Style
The design system powers an institutional enterprise SaaS platform dedicated to workplace ergonomics, movement breaks, and occupational health governance. The emotional tone balances corporate institutional trust with vitalizing, health-centric encouragement: composed, invigorating, compliant, and deeply respectful of employee time and workplace focus.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** with **Refined Tactile Ergonomics**:
- Clinical clarity without coldness: Crisp white structural planes suspended over gentle slate-tinted canvas backdrops.
- Visual cadence driven by micro-rhythms: Structured dashboard data matrices transition into fluid, soft-surfaced break timers and progress monitors.
- High accessibility standards aligned with corporate safety and occupational health governance frameworks (WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance).

## Colors
The palette balances deep naval institutional gravity with restorative biophilic accents.

- **Primary (`#1e3a8a` / `#172554`)**: Navy depths used for primary brand touchpoints, top-level navigation shells, structural emphasis, and authoritative typography. Represents institutional security and operational stability.
- **Secondary (`#10b981`)**: Emerald vitality, signaling active movement sessions, completed wellness goals, positive habit streaks, and active biometric/ergonomic pulses.
- **Tertiary (`#2563eb`)**: Cobalt electric, applied to primary interactive states, inline links, active tabs, progress loaders, and high-priority action affordances.
- **Surface Canvas (`#f8fafc`)**: Neutral slate light tone preventing retina fatigue during long workstation usage, serving as the calm foundation for pristine white container layers.
- **Container Surfaces (`#ffffff`)**: Pristine white layers providing optical elevation and distinction for charts, exercise video modules, and metric cards.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#e2e8f0`)**: Subtle structure that organizes density without visual noise.

## Typography
The system pairs **Manrope** for authoritative, geometric display headings with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body and interface mechanics.

- **Manrope** provides a modern, balanced architectural geometry. Its robust letterforms anchor data tables, executive KPI metrics, routine titles, and milestone achievements.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces subtle humanized warmth into body copy, instructional exercise cues, microcopy, and input elements, sustaining legibility across fast scanning and high-density screens.
- Numerical statistics, timers, and compliance indicators leverage tabular figure alignments (`tnum`) to eliminate spatial shifting during active countdown sequences.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a fluid 12-column responsive grid built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, with a strict 4pt baseline for micro-alignments.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid architecture, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Left-hand persistent navigation rail (64px collapsed, 260px expanded).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Navigation collapses to an ergonomic bottom or slide-over drawer.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Component stacks adapt to single-column full-width flow, prioritizing quick-trigger timers and prompt response buttons.
- Vertical density is modulated contextually: enterprise administration grids employ tight internal cell padding (`space-sm`), while routine exercise guided cards utilize breathing space (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to promote calm and focus.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Ambient Diffused Drop Shadows** tinted in deep navy rather than achromatic black.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#f8fafc` background base, entirely devoid of shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Dashboard Panels)**: `#ffffff` surface, bounded by a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`), supported by an ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards, Dropdowns & Popovers)**: `#ffffff` with enhanced elevation: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.03)`, producing clean separation above busy analytical graphs.
- **Level 3 (Exercise Timers & Modal Overlays)**: Surface floating above backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md` over `rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.4)`): `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.05)`.
- **Focus Rings**: Emphasized via offset double-rings using `#2563eb` with 2px optical white spacing.

## Shapes
The visual geometry applies **Level 2 (Rounded)** corners. This balance avoids the sterile coldness of sharp corporate software while maintaining structural discipline appropriate for HR and occupational medical oversight.

- Base interactive inputs, buttons, and badges leverage `0.5rem` (8px).
- Content cards, exercise step containers, and analytical panels use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Large active pause modal windows, timer hero circles, and session completion celebrations use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Status indicator pills and user avatars use continuous circular profiles (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid gradient or fill using `#1e3a8a` hovering to `#172554`. White text, Manrope font weight 600. Subtle navy-tinted bottom shadow for physical tactility.
- **Secondary / Active**: `#10b981` background with white text, utilized specifically for "Iniciar Pausa", "Completar Ejercicio", and positive wellness completions.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, `#1e3a8a` typography, turning to `#f1f5f9` on hover.

### Cards
- Base background `#ffffff`, 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, `border-radius: 1rem`.
- Padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) for routine presentation and `space-md` (16px) for compact dashboard KPI monitors.
- Cards feature an optional top accent line (2px) in `#10b981` for active sessions or `#2563eb` for mandatory corporate pause alerts.

### Chips & Badges
- **Active / Completed Badge**: Light emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), deep emerald label (`#047857`), pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Scheduled / Pending Badge**: Soft slate tint (`#f1f5f9`), slate text (`#475569`).
- **Urgent / Ergonomic Alert**: Soft amber tint (`#fef3c7`), amber text (`#b45309`).

### Input Fields
- Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, radius `0.5rem`, typography in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#2563eb` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 4px border radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94a3b8` over `#ffffff`.
- Checked: Solid `#10b981` (for habit validation) or `#2563eb` (for administrative filters), with crisp white check/dot glyphs.

### Specialized Wellness SaaS Components
- **Micro-Break Countdown Ring**: Circular SVG countdown with a smooth `#10b981` stroke over a `#e2e8f0` track. Centered display typography showing remaining pause duration.
- **Ergonomic Posture Illustration Shell**: Framed asset block with rounded corners (`12px`), slate background (`#f8fafc`), and high-contrast instruction text aligned underneath.
- **Departmental Compliance Bar**: Multi-segment progress track with rounded caps, contrasting navy corporate goals with completed emerald department quotas.
